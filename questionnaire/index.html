AI Acceptable Use Self-Assessment

A short check to confirm you've read and understood the AI Acceptable Use Policy. No time limit.

Before you start:

  • This runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere or logged as you go.
  • At the end you'll get a score and a short summary and the possibility to download your results
  • Answer based on the policy as written, not general assumptions about AI tools.

Result

Result summary is generated locally and only leaves your device if you choose to download or share it.
